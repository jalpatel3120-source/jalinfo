📚 Physics Behind The Animation

Important Class 12 concepts

Photon Energy

According to Planck's quantum theory, electromagnetic radiation consists of photons. The energy of one photon is:


E = hf

Work Function

The minimum energy required to remove an electron from the surface of a metal is called its work function φ.


φ = hf₀

Einstein Equation

Einstein explained the photoelectric effect using the conservation of energy.


Kmax = hf − φ

Interference

When two coherent waves overlap, the resultant intensity depends upon their phase difference.

Constructive Interference

When waves arrive in phase, their amplitudes reinforce each other and produce a bright fringe.

Destructive Interference

When waves arrive out of phase, they cancel partially or completely, producing a dark fringe.
